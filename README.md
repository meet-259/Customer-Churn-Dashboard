# Customer-Churn-Dashboard

## Project Overview
Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for subscription-based businesses because acquiring a new customer is significantly more expensive than retaining an existing one.

This project analyzes customer behavior to identify the major factors contributing to churn Python (Pandas). The goal is to uncover patterns among customers who leave the company and provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.


## Dataset Used
**Dataset**: Telco Customer Churn Dataset
The dataset includes:
- Customer demographics (Gender, Senior Citizen, Partner, Dependents)
- Account information (Tenure, Contract Type, Payment Method)
- Services subscribed (Internet Service, Online Security, Tech Support, Streaming Services)
- Billing details (Monthly Charges, Total Charges)
- Customer churn status

## Key Insights
- Overall customer churn rate is 26.5%, indicating about one in four customers leaves the company.
- Customers with Month-to-Month contracts have the highest churn, while Two-Year contracts show the strongest retention.
- Most churn occurs within the first 12 months of a customer's subscription.
- Customers with higher monthly charges are more likely to churn.
- Fiber Optic internet users have a higher churn rate than DSL users.
- Customers paying via Electronic Check churn more frequently than those using automatic payment methods.
- Customers without Online Security or Tech Support services exhibit significantly higher churn.
- Senior citizens and customers without partners or dependents show relatively higher churn rates.


## Recommendations
- Encourage customers to choose 1-year or 2-year contracts by offering discounts or special benefits, as they are less likely to leave.
- Focus on new customers during their first year by providing better support and regular follow-ups to improve their experience.
- Offer Online Security and Tech Support as affordable bundles to increase customer satisfaction and reduce churn.
- Review pricing for customers with high monthly charges and provide discounts or better plans if needed.
- Encourage customers to use automatic payment methods instead of Electronic Check by offering small rewards or cashback.
